    Where the data comes from

    Bull Superski's fluor-free glide tests in Holmenkollen (bullsuperski.no/glidprosjektet), transcribed from the report images. Each report ranks the top 3 of about 8 pairs of skis and lists the rest unranked.

    How the pick works

    Every past test is weighted by how close its snow temperature and snow humidity were to your conditions: a test 2 °C or 30 humidity points away counts about 60 %, twice as far about 14 %. Each product earns 3/2/1 points for 1st/2nd/3rd (ties share the place, combinations credit every product in them), 0 when tested but off the podium. The score is the weighted average points over all tests, so a product that was never tested in similar conditions scores close to 0.

    • Podium: share of the similar tests it was in where it finished top 3.
    • Evidence: how many similar tests it took part in (strong / some / thin).
    • How Bull applied it: method from its best result in the most similar conditions.

    How reliable is it?

    Checked by hiding each test and predicting it from the others. The real winner was in the top 3 about 50 % of the time (random guessing: 38 %), and the #1 pick won about 20–30 % of the time (random: 13 %). Simply picking the product with the best overall record does about as well — in about half the tests the winner had never been on a podium before, which no model can predict.

    Use it to pick a shortlist, include proven all-rounders (Skigo LDQ, Toko HP Blue), and test on the day if you can. Snow humidity readings differ between seasons, so if you don't know it, tick "I don't know the humidity".
